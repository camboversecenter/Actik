# Actik Trademark and Branding Policy

The Actik source code is open source under the Apache-2.0 license, and you are free to
use, modify, and self-host it under those terms. **However, the open-source code license
does not grant any rights to the Actik name, logo, or brand** (Apache-2.0 section 6
explicitly does not grant trademark rights).

## What this means

- **The code is free.** You may fork it, change it, and run your own version.
- **The name and logo are not.** "Actik", the Actik logo, and related branding are
  trademarks of the Actik project and its maintainers.

## Please do

- Use the code to build and run your own service under a **different name and logo**.
- State factually that your product is "based on Actik" or "powered by Actik", as long
  as you do not imply that the Actik project endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with
  your version.

## Please do not

- Use the Actik name or logo as the name/branding of your own fork or service.
- Use the Actik name or logo in a way that suggests official status, endorsement, or
  affiliation without written permission.
- Register domain names, social accounts, or trademarks that could confuse users into
  thinking your version is the official Actik.

## Why

Actik issues credentials people rely on. Protecting the name keeps users safe from
copycats and fake issuers while leaving the code fully open for anyone to reuse.

## Questions or permission requests

If you would like permission to use the Actik name or logo, please open an issue or
contact the maintainers.
